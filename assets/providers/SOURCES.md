# Provider badges

The DeepSeek, Codex and Z.ai silhouettes come from [Lobe Icons 2.10.0](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/tree/9752d967494e48d8f83f2808feb2b0207bf70997), distributed as @lobehub/icons-static-png. The included MIT license applies to the source artwork.

The 64 px light and dark badges retain the original brand shapes. Their ink color, margins and rounded container are adapted for the widget.
